Policy Evaluation and Panel Data

2026-11-11

Lecture scope

Build up the idea of policy evaluation, based on intuitions for casual infernece. In particular, follow the connections made by Chin, Eckles, & Ugander on seeding experiments, as a way to see how that work is a policy evaluation problem over “stochastic seeding strategies”, as opposed to an ATE between arms of an experiment.

As a second topic, develop the idea of causal inference based on panel data, following last-ten-years papers by Guido Imbens and others on such problems. Pay particular attention to causal inference methods that apply matrix completion methods to produce estimators. Describe in detail how one can interpret such matrix completion methods in terms of what it means to assume that the relevant data matrices are low rank.
